Hi, I'm Iona.

I'm an independent tattoo artist based in Berwick-upon-Tweed. I love creating tattoos that feel personal to the person wearing them, whether that's a custom idea or a favourite flash design.

My work focuses primarily on black and grey, with an interest in bold designs, illustrative detail and pieces with a little character.
