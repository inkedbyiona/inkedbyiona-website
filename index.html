I'm the founder of Inked By Iona, a self-taught tattoo artist based in Berwick-upon-Tweed.

I love bringing people's ideas to life, whether that's something small and meaningful, a bold statement piece or a design that's completely unique to you.

I love a bit of variety, so I'm always excited to hear what you've got in mind and work together to create something you'll love!

Before you book

The Important Bits.

A few useful things to know before your appointment. Click any heading to read more.

Bookings & deposits

Enquiries can be submitted through Venue Ink. For smaller tattoo appointments, the booking payment is £12: a £10 tattoo deposit deducted from your final tattoo price, plus a separate £2 Venue Ink booking fee.

Deposits for larger tattoos or longer sessions vary. The amount, payment method and any applicable fees will be explained before you confirm your booking.

Cancellations & rescheduling

Please give at least 48 hours' notice if you need to cancel or rearrange an appointment. You may reschedule up to twice using the same deposit, subject to availability. Further rescheduling may require a new deposit.

With less than 48 hours' notice, or if you don't attend, some or all of your tattoo deposit may be retained where reasonable to reflect appointment time lost and preparation or design work already completed. This is assessed fairly based on the circumstances, not as an automatic penalty.

If I need to cancel, you'll be offered an alternative appointment or a refund of the tattoo deposit. Any booking-fee refund will depend on the applicable terms and your statutory rights.

Late arrivals

Please arrive on time, and let me know as soon as possible if you're running late. If you're more than 15 minutes late without prior agreement, the appointment may need to be shortened, rearranged or cancelled depending on the time available.

If you've contacted me in advance, I'll do my best to accommodate you. Any deposit retention will depend on the circumstances and the agreed booking terms.

Custom designs & changes

Custom designs may be prepared before your appointment. Please let me know about significant changes to the design, size or placement as early as possible, ideally at least 48 hours in advance.

Major last-minute changes may require extra design time, a revised quote or a new appointment.

Age restrictions & preparation

You must be at least 18 years old to be tattooed. Valid photographic ID may be requested; parental consent does not override the legal minimum age.

Please arrive well rested, hydrated and having eaten. Avoid alcohol before your appointment, and share any relevant health or skin concerns during your consultation.

Bringing someone along

You're welcome to bring a friend, partner or family member for moral support! Guests must follow studio hygiene guidance and stay clear of the tattooing area. Numbers may need to be limited for space and safety.

Nothing in these booking policies affects your statutory consumer rights. Any charges or deposit deductions will be explained at booking and applied fairly.
